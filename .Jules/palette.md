## 2026-05-12 - Form Accessibility in Settings Tab\n**Learning:** The forms in `SettingsTab.tsx` lacked proper label associations and ARIA descriptions for helper text. Screen readers and users relying on clicking labels to focus inputs were impacted.\n**Action:** Always ensure `htmlFor` on `<label>` matches the `id` on `<Input>`. When an input has helper text underneath it, add `aria-describedby="<helper-id>"` to the input and `id="<helper-id>"` to the helper text element.
