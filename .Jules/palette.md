## 2024-07-02 - Form Field Accessibility
**Learning:** Connecting `<label>` elements to `<Input>` via `htmlFor` and `id` respectively, and using `aria-describedby` for helper text ensures that screen readers announce the field names and necessary instructions properly.
**Action:** Always add `htmlFor` and `id` to linked form elements. Provide `aria-describedby` and target helper text IDs to enrich the screen reader context.
